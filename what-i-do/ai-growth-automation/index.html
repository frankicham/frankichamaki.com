AI Growth Automation: connecting data, decisions and action

AI growth automation is the systems layer between information and action: retrieve the right data, apply rules or AI reasoning, route the output and keep a human in the loop where judgement matters.

On this pageWhat it meansThe problem it addressesHow I approach itWhat this looks like in practiceTools and systemsResults and current statusRelated work

What it means

I use automation to remove repetitive handoffs and make useful context available at the moment a person needs it. The goal is not autonomous marketing for its own sake. It is a clearer, more reliable workflow.

The problem it addresses

Growth teams often have plenty of tools but weak connective tissue. Research lives in one system, CRM history in another, follow-up in email and the decision still happens manually. Automation is useful when it joins those steps without hiding how the decision was made.

How I approach it

I start with a bounded workflow, define the inputs, decision points and output, then choose the simplest toolchain that can support it. My current work includes Claude plugins and agents, Clay, HubSpot Breeze AI agents and automation platforms including n8n, Make and Zapier.

AI Outbound & ABM: from account selection to sales sequences

AI can make outbound faster, but speed is not the strategy. I use it to improve account selection, research and personalisation while keeping targeting, relevance and sales judgement explicit.

On this pageWhat it meansThe problem it addressesHow I approach itA practical workflowTools and systemsMeasured examplesRelated work

What it means

I treat outbound and ABM as an operating system: define the ICP, decide which accounts matter, identify relevant triggers or pain hypotheses, create appropriate messaging, execute across channels and feed responses back into the model.

The problem it addresses

The common failure mode is scaling low-relevance outreach. More enrichment and more generated copy simply produce more noise if account selection, timing and offer are weak. AI should improve the quality of the decision before it increases the volume of execution.

How I approach it

In recent in-house work, I designed signal-based outbound programs in Clay with ICP scoring, tiered account lists, fit gates and AI-generated pain lines, executed as HubSpot sales sequences. I also built target lists, CRM campaigns and multi-step email, call and LinkedIn sequences across event, advisory and revenue programs.

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Define account fit and exclusions.

  2. 02

    Tier accounts and add timing or growth signals where available.

  3. 03

    Generate a pain hypothesis or research angle, then review it for plausibility.

  4. 04

    Choose the sequence and channel mix: email, call, LinkedIn or a campaign path.

  5. 05

    Measure replies, connects, meetings and downstream opportunity movement rather than optimising only for send volume.

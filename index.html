FAQ

Questions I’m often asked

Straight answers to the questions founders, hiring managers and marketing leaders care about most.

What does a go-to-market strategist do?

A go-to-market strategist connects market selection, ICP, positioning, messaging, pricing, channels, demand creation, sales alignment and measurement into one commercial system.

How do you define an ideal customer profile (ICP)?

Start with the customers most likely to receive meaningful value, buy with reasonable friction and expand over time. Combine firmographic data with buying triggers, pain, urgency, economics and evidence from real deals.

How do you build a B2B demand generation engine?

Define the ICP and buying problem, create useful content and offers, build paid and organic acquisition, connect conversion paths to CRM and automation, then improve the system through funnel analytics and experimentation.

What channels work best for B2B SaaS growth?

There is no universal winner. Search captures existing intent, LinkedIn can create and capture professional demand, email nurtures and activates, and content compounds authority. The best mix depends on ICP, ACV, sales cycle and market maturity.

What is the difference between demand generation and lead generation?

Demand generation creates awareness, preference and buying intent. Lead generation captures identifiable prospects. Good growth systems do both without mistaking form fills for real demand.

What does full-funnel demand generation include?

Positioning, content, paid and organic acquisition, landing pages, conversion, automation, scoring, nurture, sales handoff, attribution and ongoing optimisation from first touch through opportunity and revenue.

How do you measure the success of a GTM strategy?

Measure commercial movement: qualified pipeline, conversion rates, cost per opportunity, CAC, pipeline velocity, win rate, revenue contribution and retention or LTV where relevant.

How do you improve ROI from paid marketing?

Improve the whole chain rather than just bids: audience, problem, creative, message, offer, landing page, conversion, follow-up and attribution. Then test deliberately and move budget toward the combinations that create qualified pipeline.

FAQ

AI growth & GTM systems, explained

Straight answers for founders, hiring managers and marketing leaders evaluating AI-native growth capability.

Who is Franki Chamaki?

Franki Chamaki is a Sydney-based growth and demand generation leader focused on AI-powered GTM systems. He is Head of Demand Generation at Think & Grow and previously co-founded HIVERY, where he served as VP of Marketing.

What does an AI GTM systems builder do?

In my work, it means connecting account data, buying signals, AI tools, CRM workflows and marketing automation so sales and marketing teams can prioritise accounts and act on relevant information.

What tools does Franki use?

My work includes Clay, HubSpot, Claude, Apify and automation platforms such as n8n, Make and Zapier. I select tools around the workflow rather than starting with a tool and looking for a use case.

What has Franki built?

My projects at Think & Grow include Contact Intel, a pre-call intelligence plugin; a target account intelligence agent monitoring growth signals across 146 accounts; and EngineRoom, an AI-assisted event follow-up app.

How does GTM Sprint relate to Franki’s work?

GTM Sprint is my side project for running GTM experiments and supporting solo founders. It sits alongside my role at Think & Grow.

Where does Franki share his work?

I share marketing, AI and GTM lessons through this website, LinkedIn, YouTube and GTM Sprint.

Target Account Intelligence Agent

Weekly growth signals across target accounts, posted to sales in Slack.

Built in-house at a B2B growth and AI advisory firm.

What it does

The agent watches a list of sales target accounts every week. When something changes, it tells the account owner in Slack, with a short note on why it matters.

Signals it looks for:

  • Funding or capital raises
  • Fast hiring or senior job ads
  • New executives or leadership changes
  • Expansion into new markets or offices
  • Product launches, big customer wins, partnerships, M&A

Problem

Sales teams know timing matters. A company that just raised money or hired a new CRO is far more likely to take a call.

But nobody has time to check 100+ accounts every week. So signals get missed, or found weeks too late.

My role

I designed and built the agent:

  • Agreed the signals that matter with sales
  • Built the scraping, research and scoring steps in Clay
  • Added a scoring gate so only meaningful signals get through
  • Rebuilt it to run on a weekly schedule, only flag new signals, and use a lower-cost AI model

Workflow

  1. 01
    Pull accounts

    Bring target accounts in from the CRM.

  2. 02
    Scan sources

    Check news and web sources for each account every week.

  3. 03
    Score signals

    Use AI to read and score what changed.

  4. 04
    Filter noise

    Let only new signals above the score threshold through.

  5. 05
    Deliver

    Post a weekly Slack brief for the account owner.

Tools and integrations

  • Workflow and data: Clay
  • Web scraping: Apify
  • AI research and scoring: Claude
  • Source list: HubSpot
  • Delivery: Slack

Example output

A weekly Slack post listing:

  • Account name
  • The signal and its source
  • Why it matters for sales
  • A suggested angle for outreach

Results & status

  • Running across 146 target accounts
  • Rebuilt in October 2026 to make it more reliable and cheaper to run
  • Next: scale to the full target account list once signed off
  • Not yet measured: meetings or pipeline from signals

Limitations

  • Private companies often have less public news
  • AI can over-rate a weak signal, so the score gate needs tuning
  • Running on hundreds of accounts uses a lot of enrichment credits
  • A signal is a reason to reach out, not a guarantee of interest

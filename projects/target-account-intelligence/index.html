What it does

I built a target account intelligence agent at Think & Grow using Clay, Apify and Claude. It monitors 146 sales target accounts for growth signals and posts weekly briefs to Slack.

The problem it addresses

Static target-account lists age quickly. The system addresses the need to keep a defined account set under observation so sales can see recent growth signals rather than relying only on a one-time list build.

My role

I built the account-intelligence workflow and the weekly briefing process for the 146-account target set.

How the workflow works

  1. 01

    A defined set of 146 sales target accounts forms the monitoring universe.

  2. 02

    The intelligence workflow uses Clay, Apify and Claude to monitor and process growth signals.

  3. 03

    Relevant account-level context is consolidated into a recurring brief.

  4. 04

    A weekly account intelligence brief is posted to Slack for the sales team.

Tools and integrations

ClayApifyClaudeSlack

Example output

The documented output is a weekly Slack brief covering growth signals across the monitored target-account set. A real brief is not published here because no anonymised output has yet been approved for public use.

Results and current status

DOCUMENTED STATUS

Built at Think & Grow and monitoring 146 sales target accounts. The supplied CV does not state a quantified pipeline, conversion, adoption or productivity outcome from the agent.

PROPOSED / EXPECTED BENEFIT

The intended benefit is to help sales focus attention on accounts where new information may indicate relevance or timing. That is the purpose of the workflow, not a published causal result.

Limitations and lessons learned

A growth signal is not the same as buying intent. Signals need context and human interpretation, and monitoring quality depends on the source coverage available for each account. No quantified downstream impact is published yet.

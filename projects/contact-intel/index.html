Contact Intel

AI pre-call briefs for sales, delivered in Slack.

Built in-house at a B2B growth and AI advisory firm.

On this pageWhat it doesProblemMy roleWorkflowToolsExample outputResults & statusLimitationsRelated projects

What it does

A sales rep types a name, an email, or snaps a photo of a badge or business card. Contact Intel sends back a short brief:

  • Who the person is and their role
  • Past conversations and deal history from the CRM
  • Recent company and industry news
  • Growth signals, like funding, hiring or new leaders
  • A relevant case study and a few conversation openers

Reps choose Quick (under a minute, for right before a call or at an event) or Full (a few minutes, pulling from more sources).

Problem

Good reps prep before every call. Busy reps don't have time.

The information exists, but it's spread across the CRM, email, meeting notes, shared drives and the news. Pulling it together takes 15-20 minutes, so it gets skipped. Then the first five minutes of the call are spent catching up.

My role

I designed and built it:

  • Defined what a useful brief looks like, with input from sales
  • Built it as a Claude plugin and connected it to the CRM, enrichment and internal sources
  • Designed the Quick vs Full options for different situations
  • Set it up in Slack so reps can use it where they already work

Workflow

  1. 01
    Input

    Send a name, email or badge photo to Claude in Slack.

  2. 02
    Choose depth

    Pick Quick for speed or Full for deeper research.

  3. 03
    CRM context

    Check the person, company and past conversations in the CRM.

  4. 04
    Signals

    Pull growth signals and recent company or sector news.

  5. 05
    Match proof

    Find a relevant case study for the conversation.

  6. 06
    Brief

    Return a short pre-call brief with conversation openers.

Tools and integrations

  • AI: Claude (plugin and skills)
  • Data: HubSpot, Clay, web news
  • Internal sources (Full mode): meeting notes, email, shared drive, data warehouse, Slack
  • Delivery: Slack

Example output

A brief in Slack with:

  • Person and company snapshot
  • Last touchpoints with the business
  • 2-3 recent signals or news items
  • One relevant case study
  • 3 conversation openers

Results & status

  • Live in Slack and in testing with sales
  • Next: demo to the wider sales team and roll out
  • Not yet measured: time saved per call, usage, effect on meetings booked

Limitations

  • The brief is only as good as the CRM data behind it
  • News search can miss smaller or private companies
  • Full mode takes longer, so it's not for last-second use
  • Reps should still sense-check before quoting anything

EngineRoom

AI event follow-up, from badge scan to Gmail draft.

Built in-house at a B2B growth and AI advisory firm.

On this pageWhat it doesProblemMy roleWorkflowToolsExample outputResults & statusLimitationsRelated projects

What it does

You meet someone at an event and snap their badge. EngineRoom:

  • Reads the name and company
  • Looks them up in the CRM
  • Writes a short follow-up brief
  • Saves a ready-to-edit email draft in Gmail

A person always reviews and sends the email.

Problem

Events create great conversations, then the follow-up goes cold.

By the time the team is back at their desks, details are fuzzy, badges are in a pile, and nobody remembers who promised what. The CRM history that would make the follow-up personal isn't checked.

My role

I designed and built the app:

  • Mapped the follow-up process from badge to email
  • Built the app and connected it to the CRM and Gmail
  • Wrote the AI prompts for the brief and draft email
  • Fixed the issues that came up in testing, like sharing and draft saving

Workflow

  1. 01

    Snap a photo of the badge

  2. 02

    The app reads the name and company

  3. 03

    It checks the CRM for existing history

  4. 04

    AI writes a follow-up brief

  5. 05

    A draft email is saved to Gmail for review

Tools and integrations

  • App: React, built with Claude
  • Data: HubSpot
  • AI: Claude
  • Email: Gmail

Example output

A Gmail draft with:

  • A personal opening that references the event
  • One relevant point from CRM history or the conversation
  • A clear, low-pressure next step
Demo visual to addGmail draft screenshot using a made-up contact. Demo data only - no real client or contact names.

Results & status

  • Built and tested
  • Not yet measured: time saved, reply rate or meetings booked

Limitations

  • Drafts still need a human check for tone and accuracy
  • CRM data can be incomplete
  • It can't capture what was said in the conversation unless you add a note

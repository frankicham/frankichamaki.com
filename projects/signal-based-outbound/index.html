Signal-Based Outbound Engine

Find the right accounts, learn what's changing for them, and write a first email that actually fits.

Built in-house at a B2B growth and AI advisory firm, for a B2B technology client.

On this pageWhat it doesProblemMy roleWorkflowToolsExample outputResults & statusLimitationsRelated projects

What it does

The engine runs outbound from start to finish, with very little manual work:

  • Scores companies against the client's ideal customer profile (ICP)
  • Learns the client's value and best use cases, so it knows what to pitch
  • Researches each target company and its recent news
  • Spots a likely challenge, like a product launch or a new executive who needs early wins
  • Writes a short, personal email: subject under 8 words, body around 88 words
  • Saves it to the CRM record, ready for the sales sequence

Problem

Most cold email fails for one of two reasons. It goes to the wrong company, or it says nothing about what's going on for them.

Personal research fixes both, but it takes 15-20 minutes per account. Nobody can do that for hundreds of accounts, so teams fall back to templates and get ignored.

My role

I designed and built the full pipeline:

  • Set up ICP scoring, account tiers and "fit gates" so weak-fit accounts drop out early
  • Wrote the AI prompts that learn the client's value, read the news and suggest the likely pain point
  • Set the email rules: short, specific, one clear ask, no hype
  • Connected it to HubSpot so each email lands on the right contact record
  • Managed the sales sequences once contacts were loaded

Workflow

  1. 01
    Find

    Pull target companies and score them against the ICP.

  2. 02
    Filter

    Fit gates remove poor matches before any credits are spent.

  3. 03
    Learn

    An AI agent studies the client's value and use cases.

  4. 04
    Research

    Read each target's recent news and signals.

  5. 05
    Diagnose

    Name the most likely challenge behind that signal.

  6. 06
    Write

    Draft a subject line and an 88-word email linking the challenge to the client's help.

  7. 07
    Load

    Save the email to the HubSpot contact and enrol it in a sales sequence.

Tools and integrations

Results & status

  • Built and used to run live outbound sequences
  • Also used to enrich trade show exhibitor lists for event outreach
  • Not yet measured publicly: reply rate, meetings booked, pipeline

Limitations

  • If the news is thin, the email gets generic, so those accounts need a human touch
  • AI can misread a signal, so a person should check emails before they send
  • Enrichment credits add up fast at scale, which is why the fit gates come first
  • Outreach has to follow local spam and privacy rules

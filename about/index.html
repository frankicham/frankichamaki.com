What I do now

At Think & Grow, I work across AI, automation, account-based marketing, signal-based outbound, demand generation, SEO/AEO and analytics. The common thread is building practical systems that help sales and marketing teams make better decisions and act on better information.

How I think about AI and growth

I’m more interested in workflows than demos. AI is most useful when it is connected to real data, clear rules, a commercial decision and a human who knows what good looks like.

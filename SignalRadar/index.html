ABOUT SIGNAL RADAR

A better reason
to reach out.

Signal Radar helps Respondent's sales team identify companies with a timely, evidence-based need for verified participants and subject-matter experts.

What it does

Rather than searching for candidates, it monitors publicly accessible hiring activity to find organisations expanding UX research, insights, research operations, and human AI evaluation. These are potential demand signals for Respondent's panel.

1
Discover
Perplexity and OpenAI search for public hiring evidence, including indexed job boards and company careers pages.
2
Verify and enrich
Collect company names, domain, roles, LinkedIn company page where verified, source URL and posting date where available.
3
Prioritise
Score Fit (35%), Intent (30%) and Momentum (35%) to explain why an AE should investigate now.
4
Act
Open original job listings, inspect supporting evidence, and export qualified accounts to CSV for AE review.

Designed for Respondent

Who we find: teams hiring UX Researchers, Heads of Insights, Research Ops, Human Data, Model Evaluators, RLHF specialists and expert reviewers.

Why it matters: hiring suggests research capacity or new evaluation programs. The outreach hypothesis is that these teams may need verified respondents or hard-to-recruit specialists.

What we never claim: a job post alone does not prove budget, product usage or active buying intent. Dates, LinkedIn profiles and roles are not fabricated if the source doesn't confirm them.
